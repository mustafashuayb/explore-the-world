Welcome To Explore The World

Have you ever wondered what our world is like? No? Well, then this website was created especially for you!

On this website, I will show you different images of countries and continents, along with a table to help you compare the differences between countries. You will also get to explore different cultures, landmarks, foods, languages, and interesting facts from around the world.

So, what are you waiting for? Let's explore the world!

Continents

There are seven continents in the world:

  • Africa
  • Asia
  • Europe
  • North America
  • South America
  • Australia
  • Antarctica

Countries and Cultures

Every country has its own unique culture, including different foods, languages, traditions, clothing, and celebrations.

France

  • Language: French
  • Famous food: Croissants
  • Known for: The Eiffel Tower

Japan

  • Language: Japanese
  • Famous food: Sushi
  • Known for: Mount Fuji

Kenya

  • Languages: English and Swahili
  • Famous food: Ugali
  • Known for: Wildlife and national parks

United States

  • Language: English
  • Famous food: Burgers
  • Known for: The Statue of Liberty

India

  • Languages: Hindi and English
  • Famous food: Biryani
  • Known for: The Taj Mahal

Australia

  • Language: English
  • Famous food: Meat pie
  • Known for: The Sydney Opera House
